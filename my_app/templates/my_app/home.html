OUR CLASSES

At Fairford Gym, we offer a diverse range of exercise programs tailored to suit your fitness goals and preferences. Whether you're looking to improve cardiovascular health, build strength, or simply have fun while working out, we have something for everyone. Explore our offerings below:

Boxing

Unleash your inner fighter with our boxing classes, where you'll learn proper techniques, improve coordination, and enhance endurance while getting an incredible full-body workout.

Aerobics

Join our high-energy aerobics classes designed to boost cardiovascular efficiency, burn calories, and improve overall fitness levels through dynamic and engaging routines.

HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)

Experience the power of HIIT workouts, featuring short bursts of intense exercises alternated with brief recovery periods. Maximize calorie burn, increase metabolism, and challenge your limits in these efficient and effective sessions.

Step Board Workout

Step up your fitness game with our step aerobics classes, utilizing a raised platform to elevate your heart rate, improve coordination, and sculpt muscles through rhythmic stepping patterns.

Taebo

Blend martial arts movements with cardio exercises in our exhilarating Taebo classes, offering a unique fusion of strength training, flexibility, and cardiovascular conditioning.

Zumba

Dance your way to fitness with our Zumba classes, combining Latin-inspired dance moves with aerobic elements for a fun and effective full-body workout. Improve endurance, coordination, and mood while grooving to lively rhythms.

Yoga

Find balance, flexibility, and inner peace with our yoga classes. Explore various styles, from Vinyasa to Hatha, as you improve strength, focus, and mindfulness through breath-centered movements and relaxation techniques.

Full Body Workout

Experience the ultimate in comprehensive training with our full-body workout sessions. Target multiple muscle groups across the upper body, lower body, and core for a balanced and efficient exercise regimen.
